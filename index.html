FAQ

Common questions

Where does the refund come from?+

A pooled refund layer funded by protocol partners and MEV rebates redistributes a share of already-paid gas back to active wallets. You're not buying anything — you're reclaiming value from fees you already spent.

Do I sign anything that moves funds?+

No. Connecting is read-only. The only transaction you sign is the claim itself, which pulls the refund into your wallet. GetMyGas never has permission to move your existing tokens.

Which chains are supported?+

Ten EVM networks including Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism, Base, Polygon, BNB Chain, Avalanche, zkSync Era, Linea and Scroll. Refunds are calculated per chain and combined.

How is my tier decided?+

By total transaction count and cumulative gas spent across all supported chains. The more you've transacted historically, the higher your refund percentage — from Bronze up to Diamond.

Is there a fee?+

A small service fee plus the network cost of the claim is deducted from the refund. The refund is only shown if the net result is positive, so claiming always leaves you ahead.
