Connection

Pairs stream from the dexscreener public API for the chain you pick. The chip above the table shows whether the feed is live.

Spam names, unreadable symbols and zero-price pairs are always filtered, in both modes.

dexscreener has no "list every pair on this chain" endpoint. Give it one token address from the chain and the terminal pulls every pair that token trades in, then keeps discovering from there.

Feed status: waiting for the first request.

Leave the default or point it at your own node. A private endpoint usually lands you a block earlier.

Wallet safety

Every trade is signed by your own wallet in its own window. The terminal never asks for a seed phrase or a private key — if any page or person asks you for one, it is not us.

Stopping the bot leaves open positions untouched. You can always close them by hand from the dashboard.
